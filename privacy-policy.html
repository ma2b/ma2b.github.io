Mindwalker — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 12, 2026

Overview

Mindwalker is an immersive exploration game developed by Yufei Ma. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data Collection

Mindwalker does not collect, transmit, or share your personal data with the developer or third parties. The app may save limited settings locally on your device so your preferences persist between sessions. Specifically:

  • No account registration or login required
  • No personal information collected (name, email, phone, etc.)
  • No location data collected
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No cookies or similar tracking technologies used by the app
  • No camera, microphone, or photo library access
  • Settings such as language, audio, graphics, and gameplay preferences may be stored locally on your device only

Device Features

The app may use the following device features, all processed locally on your device:

  • Accelerometer — used for tilt-based movement controls
  • Haptic feedback / vibration — may be used for walking-related feedback if supported by the platform and enabled in the app settings

No data from these sensors is recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Third-Party SDKs

Mindwalker uses the following third-party components to provide app functionality. In the current implementation, they run locally within the app and are not configured to collect personal data through Mindwalker:

  • Wwise (Audiokinetic) — audio engine, runs entirely offline
  • zylann.voxel — terrain generation, open-source (MIT), runs entirely offline

Network Access

As currently configured, Mindwalker does not require an internet connection and does not communicate with our servers.

Children's Privacy

Mindwalker is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us in connection with the app, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact:
myflytop@gmail.com

Mindwalker — 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 4 月 12 日

概述

Mindwalker 是由 Yufei Ma 开发的沉浸式探索游戏。本政策说明应用如何处理您的信息。

数据收集

Mindwalker 不会向开发者或第三方收集、传输或共享您的个人数据。应用可能仅在您的设备本地保存少量设置，以便在不同会话之间保留您的偏好。具体而言：

  • 无需注册或登录账号
  • 不收集任何个人信息（姓名、邮箱、电话等）
  • 不收集位置数据
  • 不进行使用分析或追踪
  • 无广告，不使用广告标识符
  • 应用不使用 Cookie 或类似追踪技术
  • 不访问相机、麦克风或相册
  • 语言、音频、图形和玩法偏好等设置可能仅保存在您的设备本地

设备功能

应用可能使用以下设备功能，所有数据均在本地处理：

  • 加速度计 — 用于倾斜移动控制
  • 触觉反馈 / 振动 — 在平台支持且您在应用设置中启用时，可用于与步行相关的反馈

这些传感器数据不会被记录、存储或传输。

第三方 SDK

Mindwalker 使用以下第三方组件来提供应用功能。在当前实现中，它们均在应用内本地运行，且未被配置为通过 Mindwalker 收集个人数据：

  • Wwise（Audiokinetic）— 音频引擎，完全离线运行
  • zylann.voxel — 地形生成，开源（MIT 协议），完全离线运行

网络访问

按当前配置，Mindwalker 不需要网络连接，也不会与我们的服务器通信。

儿童隐私

Mindwalker 的设计目的并非收集儿童的个人信息。如果您认为某名儿童通过本应用向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们处理。

政策变更

如本政策有更新，修订版本将发布在本页面，并更新生效日期。

联系方式
